What it does

Three things, done properly.

No account, no subscription, no signal required. Wirenut works in a basement with the phone in airplane mode.

Lookup

Plain-language search across the index. The article number is the hero — no wall of text to hunt through. Every result says which NEC edition it was written against.

Code Hunt

The journeyman exam is open book. The hard part isn't knowing the answer — it's reaching the right article under time pressure. Code Hunt times you, and keeps the ones you miss.

Calculators

Voltage drop for single and three phase, Ohm's law, AWG to mm², horsepower to kilowatts, °F to °C. Results as you type, and it tells you when a number can't be real.

It's an index, not the code.

Wirenut gets you to the right page. Your code book has the final word — and the app says so on every screen, not in a footnote.

That line is the whole design. An index that pretends to be the code is worse than no index at all, because you stop checking.

Straight answers

Before you hand over an email.

What do I actually get right now?

The state-by-state edition chart as a PDF, sent once it's finished — every line carries the source it came from, so you can verify it rather than trust it. Nothing else lands in your inbox until the app ships.

When does the app come out?

Target is late October 2026, on iOS first. If it slips, you'll hear the new date from us before you hear silence.

Subscription?

No. One payment unlocks the full index, and it stays unlocked. Search and the calculators work before you pay anything.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. The index ships inside the app. No account, no login, nothing to load — which is the point, because service in a mechanical room is a coin flip.

Which NEC edition is the index written against?

NEC 2023, and every screen tells you so. That covers the largest group of states by a wide margin — but adoption is state by state, a few are still enforcing 2020, and three have moved to 2026. That's exactly what the free chart is for: knowing where you stand before you trust any index, including this one.
