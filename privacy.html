Privacy Policy

Wirenut is built by one developer and has no advertising business. This page says exactly what leaves your phone, and what doesn't. If a line here is ever wrong, that's a bug — tell us and it gets fixed.

1. The short version

The app has no accounts and no analytics. The only thing that leaves your device is your purchase record, handled by Apple and our purchase provider, and it is never linked to your name or email. If you join the waitlist on this website, we hold your email address until launch.

2. The app

What the app collects

DataLinked to youUsed to track youWhy
Purchase historyNoNoTo know whether you have unlocked the full index, and to restore it on a new phone
Everything elseNot collected — no name, no email, no location, no contacts, no usage analytics, no advertising identifier

Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat assigns your installation an anonymous identifier that it generates itself. Wirenut never calls the function that would attach your identity to it, so that identifier is not connected to an email address, a name, or an account — because there is no account to connect it to.

What the app does not do

  • No tracking. Nothing is shared with data brokers or advertising networks, and the app does not ask for permission to track you, because it does not.
  • No analytics. There is no Firebase, no crash reporting service, no event logging of what you search for.
  • No account. You never create one, so there is nothing to breach.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. The app does not request these permissions.

Your searches stay on the phone

The article index ships inside the app. Searching, saving an article, and running a calculator all happen on your device with no network request — which is why the whole app works in airplane mode. What you look up is not visible to us, because it never reaches us.

3. This website

The waitlist

If you enter your email address on this site, we store it for one purpose: to send you the free state-by-state NEC edition chart and to tell you when the app is released. The signup form is run by Tally, which stores submissions on our behalf; the address then sits with our email provider until launch.

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone.
  • Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • After launch, addresses that have not opted into anything further are deleted.
  • Ask us to delete your address at any time and we will, without asking why.

Fonts and hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address when it serves a page. The signup form is loaded from Tally (tally.so), so Tally also receives your IP address as soon as the page loads, before you open the form. The typefaces are served from this site itself, not from Google. Receiving an IP address is part of how the web works, and it is the same for nearly every site you visit. This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics, so there is no visitor tracking of ours to opt out of.

4. Children

Wirenut is a professional reference tool for electricians and apprentices. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

5. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to erase it. In practice this is a short list — an email address if you joined the waitlist, and an anonymous purchase record if you bought the app. Write to the address below and we will answer within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the legal basis for holding your waitlist email is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing. If you are in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

6. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the effective date at the top changes with it, and anyone on the waitlist is told by email rather than left to notice.
