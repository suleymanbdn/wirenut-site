Support

Get help with Wirenut

Wirenut is built and supported by one developer. Write in and you get an answer from the person who wrote the app, usually within two business days.

Contact

Email suleymanbudun09@gmail.com — Suleyman Budun. Questions, bug reports and corrections all go to the same place.

Restore a purchase

Wirenut Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID, so there is no account to sign in to. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the unlock screen (the Hunt tab, the Saved tab, or any locked article) and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A wrong or missing article number

If Wirenut sends you to the wrong section, tell us what you searched for, which NEC edition you had selected, and the section you expected. Every report is checked against the code and fixed in the next update. Always confirm against the edition adopted in your jurisdiction — Wirenut points you to the right page, it does not replace your code book.

Does it need internet?

No. Search, Code Hunt, the calculators and your saved list all work in airplane mode. The only step that needs a connection is buying or restoring Wirenut Pro.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. The details are in the privacy policy.
